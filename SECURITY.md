# Security policy

Nokkela-IT-Concept GmbH welcomes reports of security issues in its services, websites and
published software.

## Reporting

Send your report to **security@nokkela-it-concept.com**.

This is the address published in the company's
[security.txt](https://nokkela-it-concept.gmbh/.well-known/security.txt) (RFC 9116), which is
the authoritative source. Reports are read in German and English.

Helpful in a report: what is affected (URL, host, repository), how the issue can be reproduced,
what impact you expect, and how you would like to be credited.

Please do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability, and please do not test in ways
that degrade a service or touch other people's data.

## Scope

This policy applies to the repositories of this organisation and to the services and websites
operated by Nokkela-IT-Concept GmbH, including the `nokkela.*` domains.

## Response

Enquiries are usually answered within one business day. There is no bug bounty programme.
